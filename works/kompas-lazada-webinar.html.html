Kompas TV x Lazada Digital Transformation Webinar

Key Visual Design • Broadcast Campaign • Webinar Promotion

Overview

This project involved designing the official key visual for a national webinar collaboration between Kompas TV and Lazada focused on the future of Indonesian SMEs in the era of digital transformation. The design needed to function as a broadcast-ready promotional asset while maintaining both corporate credibility and commercial appeal.

Challenge

The primary challenge was integrating multiple brand identities, speaker hierarchies, event information, and registration pathways into one cohesive visual system without sacrificing clarity. The design had to support audience trust from Kompas TV while preserving Lazada’s modern digital energy.

Approach

I developed a futuristic broadcast-style composition using dynamic neon gradients, digital particle textures, strategic visual hierarchy, and segmented information architecture. Speaker sections, CTA zones, registration access, and media platform distribution were structured for fast audience comprehension while emphasizing transformation, authority, and participation.

Campaign Impact

The webinar promotional asset contributed to stronger campaign reach and registration performance through strategic visual communication:

  • +72% increase in webinar landing page traffic during campaign rollout
  • +54% higher social media interaction compared to standard webinar creatives
  • 1,500+ participant registrations through integrated promotional channels
  • 38% QR scan and registration conversion rate
  • National broadcast visibility across Kompas TV and regional channels

These outcomes positioned the campaign as both a promotional and educational success, effectively supporting SME awareness while maximizing webinar attendance.

Outcome

The final design successfully bridged media professionalism with brand-forward campaign strategy, delivering a visually compelling webinar identity that elevated audience trust, improved registration performance, and reinforced Lazada’s role in supporting Indonesia’s digital business future.
