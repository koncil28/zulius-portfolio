Campaign Alpha

Brand Strategy • Campaign Development

Overview

Campaign Alpha was designed to reposition a declining brand by creating a cohesive digital narrative across multiple touchpoints, focusing on clarity, consistency, and audience trust.

Challenge

The brand suffered from fragmented messaging and declining engagement. Audiences lacked a clear understanding of its value, resulting in low retention and weak brand recall.

Approach

I developed a structured campaign framework aligning visual identity, messaging, and content strategy. Each touchpoint was designed to guide users through a clear narrative journey.

Outcome

The campaign improved engagement by 45%, strengthened brand recall, and established a consistent digital presence within three months.
